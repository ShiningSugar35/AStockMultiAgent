# Workflow — Governance & Management Quality

## When to use

Use when the ready Research Team `governance-management-quality` task requires a `GovernanceManagementQualityPack`, or when ownership, incentives, related parties, audit opinions, capital allocation, dilution, pledges, succession or disclosure quality can change a company thesis.

Primary skill: `$governance-management-quality`; it feeds catalyst, Fundamental, Valuation, Bull/Bear, Reviewer and Committee tasks.

## Flow

1. Read `research-team-status <plan_id>` and consume only the ready GOVERNANCE task. Preserve `as_of` and company acquisition lineage.
2. Freeze ownership/control, board, incentive, related-party, capital-allocation, financing, dividend/buyback, M&A, audit/opinion, correction, pledge and formal enforcement records from primary sources.
3. Separate documented facts from interpretation. Compare stated policy with time-stamped actions and execution against prior disclosed targets; retain favorable and adverse evidence symmetrically.
4. Map each material finding once into a forecast/valuation mechanism and mark where it is already reflected, preventing duplicate discounts.
5. Register `GovernanceManagementQualityPack` through `research-team-role-output`, then complete with `research-team-task-result`. `GOVERNANCE_QUALITY=true` requires current ownership/control, related-party, audit/opinion and capital-allocation lineage.
6. Unresolved allegation or conflicting formal records route to `$evidence-investigation`; they do not become a fraud conclusion.

## Stop conditions

- Stop or abstain when material control, related-party or audit evidence is unavailable or conflicting.
- Do not infer intent, personality or integrity from style or rumor.
- Do not use future enforcement outcomes in historical research.
- Do not create recommendation or execution authority.
